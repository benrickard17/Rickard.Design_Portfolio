HOW IT WORKS

Fading the ink, session by session.

A Q-switched Nd:YAG laser delivers very short pulses that break the ink into tiny particles, which your body clears gradually after each session. Black and dark blue ink usually respond best. Brighter colours take longer, and some may not clear completely.

BEFORE YOUR FIRST SESSION
  • Avoid sun, sunbeds and fake tan on the area for four weeks before.
  • Arrive with clean skin, free of lotion and make-up.
  • If it's for a cover-up, bring your tattoo artist's design.
