IPL Hair Removal

Long-term hair reduction on the face and body.

Light energy targets the pigment in each hair follicle to reduce regrowth. Most people need a course of six to eight sessions, spaced four to eight weeks apart, to catch each growth cycle. Results vary with skin type and hair colour.

Tattoo Removal

Fade for a cover-up, or work towards clearing it.

Short laser pulses break the ink into tiny particles that your body clears between sessions. Some tattoos fade enough for a cover-up in a few sessions, others take many more, and some colours may not clear completely. You'll get an honest view at your consultation.
