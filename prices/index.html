Your first visit is 40 minutes. The first 20 are spent going through your scar, your surgery or injury and your medical history. The next 20 are your first treatment. After that, each session is 40 to 60 minutes and costs £80 to £120, depending on the length. How many you need depends on your scar.

Price questions, answered.

The small print, kept simple.

Yes. For IPL hair removal, tattoo removal and skin rejuvenation, the £30 for your consultation and patch test is taken off your treatment price when you book. If you decide not to book, there's nothing more to pay. Scar therapy has its own £75 first visit, which includes your first treatment.

Yes, every price on this page is for one session. Most treatments need a course, and your plan sets out how many sessions you're likely to need and the total cost before you start.

Larger areas take longer to treat, so they cost more. Each price covers your time with a registered nurse, the treatment and written aftercare.

You pay a 50% deposit by bank transfer when you book. The remaining 50% is paid by card at the clinic on the day of your treatment.

Cancel or move your appointment at least 48 hours before and your deposit is refunded in full. With less than 48 hours' notice, your appointment is cancelled and your deposit is kept. The full details are in the cancellation policy.
