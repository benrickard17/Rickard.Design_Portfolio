Most people describe it as a warm snap, like a rubber band, lasting a fraction of a second. The face, underarms and bikini line are more sensitive than the legs or back.

MORE ABOUT IPL HAIR REMOVAL →

Most people need 6 to 8 sessions of IPL hair removal, 4 to 8 weeks apart, with 1 or 2 top-ups a year. The face, neck, back and chest can take up to 12.

Tattoo removal varies more. Your plan confirms the number before you start.

COMPARE ALL TREATMENTS →

Most tattoos can be faded and many cleared, but complete removal can't be promised. Black and dark inks respond best, while bright colours like green, yellow and light blue are harder to shift.

You'll get an honest view at your consultation, including whether fading for a cover-up is the better option.
